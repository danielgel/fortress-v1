      Fortress

      Guide your dwarves to survive. Designate work; idle dwarves pick it up, eat, drink and sleep by themselves.

      Designate

      D dig  V stairs down  C chop  G gather  F fish  H hunt  T teleport  X cancel

      Drag a rectangle with the mouse, or press Enter twice with the keyboard cursor. Esc leaves the tool.

      Build & make

      B build walls, furniture and workshops ([ ] pick what)  R remove it again. O work orders tell workshops what to make.

      Tools

      Mining needs a pick, chopping an axe, fishing a rod, hunting a spear. Dwarves grab one from the stockpile. Smelt ore into metal and forge more.

      Dwarves

      The five bars are food, drink, rest, health, mood; full is good. Beds, tables and chairs, cooked meals, booze and statues keep dwarves happy, and happy dwarves work faster.

      Tips

      The surface is level 0. To go underground, dig stairs on the surface, then dig rooms on the level below. Doors keep goblins out.

      Move

      ↑↓←→ move cursor  Shift+arrows pan camera  <> or wheel change level  right-drag to pan

      Time

      Space pause  1–4 speed

      Game

      Tab next dwarf  P save  L load  N new world. The game autosaves each season.

      Work orders

      Queue items, or set keep to restock automatically. Idle dwarves do the work at a matching workshop (build one with B).
